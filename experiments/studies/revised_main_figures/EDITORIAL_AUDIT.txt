AbDev-X manuscript rewrite -- editorial and evidence audit
Prepared 10 November 2026

DELIVERABLE
AbDevX_rewritten.pdf is the complete rewritten manuscript and supplementary
material. AbDevX_rewritten.tex is the editable source. Fifteen figure assets
are included: six main figures and nine supplementary figures. Eleven panels
were rebuilt as vector plots/schematics; four supplied explanatory/GP assets
were retained with scope-specific captions. The original manuscript was not
overwritten. No new model training or experimental results were invented.

SCIENTIFIC POSITIONING
The strongest supported claim is the highest recorded mean held-out Spearman
correlation among the evaluated same-five-assay strategies: 0.471534 versus
0.451515 for D-Stack, 0.443651 for E1, 0.306506 for ESM-2 and 0.272924 for
AbLang2. The comparison concerns HIC, AC-SINS, PR-CHO, SMAC and HAC. It is not a
claim of universal state-of-the-art performance or superiority on every assay.
The complete ten-assay mean (approximately 0.298) and weak endpoints remain
prominent. This five-assay grouping is identified as descriptive/post hoc.

SUBSTANTIVE CORRECTIONS FROM THE ORIGINAL DRAFT
1. Top-level weights in results/aw_moe.csv are 0.25 for every expert on every
   primary assay. Equal-weight and constrained learned-weight results match.
   scripts/final_evaluation.py explicitly averages experts. The rewrite does
   not credit a demonstrated nonuniform final gate for the measured gain.
2. The optimization code uses 0.1 * sum(w**2), not an unsquared L2 penalty.
   Rank correlation is locally constant; a local SLSQP run remaining uniform
   is not proof that its weights are globally optimal.
3. There are six conceptual feature families but five E1 implementation
   blocks. Structural/surface features are consolidated into the bio block.
4. The evaluated cached AbLang2 external array is (80,480), not (80,960).
5. The operational metadata block has five HC/LC subtype categories. Explicit
   germline gene one-hot encoding was not established by the primary code.
6. The A-MGFS primary code applies keyword masks and ridge preprocessing. The
   claimed 30-feature greedy cap is not implemented there. Recorded retained
   counts include 39,31,122,22,22. The historical label is retained and clarified.
7. F-MoE eligible feature groups vary by assay. It is not a fixed four-group
   neural sample-level router. Charge, surface, hydrophobicity and CDR groups
   are examples; stability and other candidate families occur in the code.
8. The primary D-Stack uses two stream predictions. The old seven-prediction
   ridge stack is a distinct experiment. Its scores are labeled separately.
9. Primary four-expert code uses ridge pipelines. LightGBM/XGBoost belong to
   additional source/proxy/learner experiments, not every primary expert.
10. The actual loader reads official GDPa1 fold columns. The unsupported
    new-CD-HIT/70%-identity description was removed; the benchmark describes
    90%-identity grouping. Isotype stratification is not held-out-isotype CV.
11. GDPa3 has 80 total antibodies but HIC n=79, HAC n=61, Tm2 n=74.
12. HAC is heparin-affinity chromatography, not high-affinity aggregation
    chromatography. SMAC and SEC readout descriptions were corrected.
13. GDPa is Ginkgo Datapoints, not a CACHE dataset. The source citation was
    replaced with the verified PROPHET-Ab and Ginkgo competition publications.
14. Published champion mapping is AC-SINS 0.337 and PR-CHO 0.356. Several local
    tables transpose these. The rewrite follows the published mapping.
15. The strongest individual primary expert is D-Stack at 0.451515. The final
    ensemble does not win every assay. It also exceeds the strongest recorded
    tabular configuration mean (0.456) only modestly; some tabular configurations
    win individual endpoints.
16. The statement that all learning curves rise monotonically is false in the
    saved table. Tm2 peaks at 60% and declines. Other curves have local reversals.
17. Cross-assay arithmetic means, fold SD, cross-assay SD, and seed SD are
    distinguished. Deterministic repeated runs are not independent replication.
18. SHAP, descriptor IG, residue IG, and structural maps are labeled by their
    proxy/component model. Metric confidence intervals are not individual
    antibody prediction intervals.

COMPARISONS REQUIRING CARE BEFORE SUBMISSION
- The stored older paired-significance differences do not match the current
  primary point estimates. They are not used to claim primary pairwise
  significance. Recompute paired tests from archived predictions if needed.
- The 0.229 versus 0.139 Jiang comparison is not traceable to a fully matched
  protocol here and was removed from the main superiority claim. The real
  Jiang paper already studies assay-level modeling.
- The local DELPHI script binarizes labels, uses Transformer-LM, selects a
  numeric output column, and correlates it directly with raw endpoints. Score
  direction and selected output column need verification. For lower-is-better
  assays, pass probability has opposite orientation to raw assay values. The
  experiment is not an equal-objective regression reproduction of the platform.
- Unverified Amini/KitAb bibliography/numbers were omitted from quantitative
  superiority claims.
- A separate stored DELPHI external-pretraining table explicitly reports
  absolute correlation; it cannot support a signed matched-data comparison.
- The GP audit's raw numeric table is unavailable in the inspected results
  directory. Its old text conflicts about which PLM features are included.
  Existing panels and reported values are preserved only as an exploratory
  supplementary audit, excluded from the primary claim. Reconcile before
  treating GP results as confirmatory or fully reproducible.
- The nonprimary GDPa5 HIC value differs by run (0.143 in old prose versus
  0.162497 in the saved transfer table). The rewrite follows the saved table.
- Bispecific arm predictions share parents, some in GDPa1. Parent-disjoint
  testing and parent-aware uncertainty are needed for independent transfer claims.
- Entire selection/stacking/fusion evaluation should be nested prospectively.
  The retrospective result does not justify claiming a single inspection of
  GDPa3 labels or a new blinded benchmark win.
